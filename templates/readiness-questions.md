# Questions for the first MM workshops

Stuff I want answered early. Saves a lot of surprises later.

## Organisation

- which company codes, plants, storage locations are in scope? any to be merged or closed?
- one go-live or waves?
- any plants with warehouse management (WM / EWM)?

## Master data

- how many active materials and suppliers? how many are actually used (last 2-3 years)?
- who owns master data today - central team or each plant?
- any MDM tool or supplier portal creating vendors?
- vendors that are also customers?
- known duplicate problems?
- batch management, serial numbers in use?

## Processes

- which procurement types: stock, consumption, services, subcontracting, consignment, STO, third party?
- release strategy for PR / PO - how many levels, which criteria?
- contracts, scheduling agreements in use?
- EDI / Ariba with suppliers?
- invoice process - MIRO by AP, OCR / VIM, ERS?
- tolerances - who decided the current values, are they still right?

## Inventory / valuation

- price control S or V per material type?
- material ledger active today?
- storage location MRP in use? (has to change to MRP areas)
- physical inventory - annual, cycle counting?

## Custom stuff

- custom reports and transactions in MM - which are used?
- enhancements in PO, GR, invoice, MRP?
- custom forms for PO / RFQ?
- interfaces in and out of MM?

## History

- how much history do users need to see? (old POs, material docs)
- audit / legal requirements for history?
